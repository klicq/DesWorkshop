# Alex Morgan — portfolio design brief

## Role analysis

Source: https://tyk.io/jobs/freelance-designer/ (reviewed 14 November 2026).
The listing emphasises versatile visual production across print, events, digital, diagrams and motion; Adobe tools and Figma; AI-assisted work within established design systems; supplier collaboration and prioritisation. Technical B2B communication and web/CMS knowledge are useful extras. The engagement is remote in Europe, approximately two days weekly for six months. The culture values independence, respect, experimentation and accountability. A bold but legible visual language is an interpretation of these needs, not a specified aesthetic in the listing.

## Audience and argument

Reader: a marketing lead assessing whether a freelance designer can take over production with minimal onboarding.
Desired action: review relevant work, then start an email conversation.

1. Hero: Alex makes technical stories understandable → a direct description, engagement availability and work link.
2. Selected work: a single idea survives multiple formats → original campaign, guide and conference artwork, with detailed expandable case studies.
3. Capabilities: production can be handed off reliably → exact deliverables, tools, preflight and review practices.
4. About and experience: independent without working in isolation → a fictional career history and practical collaboration approach.
5. Contact: an easy next step → email link, copy address, downloadable sample CV.

## Art direction

Design-taste draw: “The name as a monument”, seed 471762079. Gambarino display with General Sans text; bone, chocolate and green palette. Tight equal outer margins and the designer's name spanning the page.
Idea: Alex Morgan's sample cross-format campaign system becomes the portfolio's demonstration: the same branching mark moves between a launch campaign, developer guide and conference materials.
Live interaction: switch campaign format between a landing page, social ad and event poster. The first artwork remains visible without JavaScript.
Redraws: the initial ring hero requires 18–24 distinct project images, which this three-project brief does not supply. The replacement portrait hero also lacks a real portrait, so the higher-priority monumental-name composition is used directly. The work mosaic expects 9–16 projects; replaced with oversized project visuals and a compact editorial text cluster. The about drop-cap uses a project detail instead of an invented human portrait.

## Copy deck

Hero: Alex Morgan. Independent visual designer making complex technology feel clear, human and worth a closer look. See selected work. Based in Bristol, working remotely. Two days a week, room for a six-month collaboration.
Work: Good ideas, out in the world. Three concept projects spanning brand systems, print, digital and motion.
Project: Orbit / A connected launch. An API platform's technical story, translated into a campaign that works from the first social impression to the demo page.
Project: Fieldguide / Less jargon. More understanding. A practical API guide designed to be read, used and shared, on screen and in print.
Project: Assemble / A meeting of minds. A developer conference identity that travels from a small social tile to a very large room.
Capabilities: One designer. Many formats. I pick up the system you already have, find what needs doing, and make it easier for the next person.
About: A little about me. I'm Alex, a visual designer with a soft spot for hard-to-explain things. I turn technical ideas into useful, approachable design, then build the templates that keep the work consistent.
Contact: Let's make it clear. Need a designer who can pick up your brand and get moving? I'd love to hear what you're working on.

All identities, projects, employers, dates, deliverables and availability are fictional samples requested by the user. They are disclosed on the site and CV.
